## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a cheerful, affectionate, and deeply reassuring atmosphere centered around co-learning between parents, teachers, and young children. It departs from sterile enterprise admin dashboards by blending soft tactile warmth with intuitive primary-school playfulness.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audiences:** Parents tracking elementary school progress, home-school tutors, kindergarten/primary school administrators, and young students reviewing weekly milestones together.
- **Emotional Hook:** Comfort, joyful achievement, gentle encouragement, and approachability. The interface should feel like opening a beautifully illustrated children’s storybook or receiving a handwritten gold-star sticker.

### Design Aesthetic: Playful Tactile & Pastel Softness
- **Soft Volumetric Surfaces:** Elevated card layers, pillowy tactile buttons, and sticker-inspired elements.
- **Friendly Geometry:** Hyper-rounded corners, pill tags, and organically styled doodle containers that eliminate all clinical edges.
- **Handcrafted Accents:** Whimsical badges, mascot-driven achievement markers, and soft outlines that feel handcrafted yet structured enough for admin-grade data presentation.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from a warm morning classroom: warm melted butter, ripe strawberry milk, fresh mint leaves, and soft vanilla custard paper.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Butter Sun & Amber Honey - `#F59E0B` / `#FEF3C7`):** The primary brand anchor representing optimism, focus, and playful curiosity. Used for primary buttons, active progress rings, and key metrics.
- **Secondary (Strawberry Peach Milk - `#FB7185` / `#FFF1F2`):** Soft, affectionate accents that add nurturing human warmth. Ideal for encouragement counters, favorites, and milestone stamps.
- **Tertiary (Sprout Mint & Sage - `#10B981` / `#ECFDF5`):** Expresses growth, homework completion, and positive reinforcement.
- **Quaternary Accent (Soft Mandarin Peach - `#FB923C` / `#FFF7ED`):** Used for warm callouts, active reminders, and joyful notice banners.
- **Canvas & Surface (Vanilla Cream - `#FFFDF7` / Card `#FFFFFF`):** Replaces harsh cold grays with creamy, relaxing paper tones that prevent eye fatigue during evening homework review sessions.
- **Neutrals (Warm Cocoa Stone - Text Primary `#44403C`, Text Muted `#A8A29E`, Borders `#F5EBE1`):** Softened brown-tinted stone neutrals retain high legibility without the sterile chill of pure cold slate.

## Typography

The typography couples the clear structural charm of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines with the bubbly, rounded strokes of **Quicksand** for reading copy and data labels.

### Typographic Principles
- **Generous Font Weights:** Body and label text begin at weight 600. Rounded letterforms require higher visual mass to prevent wash-out on pastel surfaces.
- **Elevated Line Spacing:** Roomy vertical spacing gives text blocks a clean, airy, child-friendly reading cadence.
- **Friendly Numerical Glyphs:** Numbers in statistics and scores are presented with bold, open counters to make review cards immediately digestible by both parents and children.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an unhurried, airy container grid that balances dense administrative reporting (grades, attendance, lesson plans) with toy-block structural breathing room.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12-column responsive fluid grid inside a 1440px centered shell. 2rem outer canvas margins and 1.25rem gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 1.5rem canvas margins and 1rem gutters. Sidebar collapses into a friendly bottom dock or floating drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 1rem canvas margin and 0.75rem gutters. Cards reflow into single-column vertical stacks.

### Rhythm & Padding
Spacing favors spacious inner paddings (`space-md` to `space-lg`) within cards to maintain the pillowy volume needed for this visual theme.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids clinical material elevation or harsh directional drops. Instead, visual depth relies on **cloud-like ambient shadows**, warm perimeter outlines, and tactile pressed states.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Vanilla Cream `#FFFDF7` background with subtle pastel cloud blobs or faint dot-matrix grid patterns.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-diffused, butter-tinted ambient glow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)` and an ultra-soft stroke of `1.5px solid #FDF2E9`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Buttons & Hover Cards):** Warm ambient lift: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.16)`. On click/active, elements drop down with an inset bottom-edge border simulating a tactile chunky rubber button.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Sticker Popovers, & Tooltips):** `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(120, 113, 108, 0.15)` with a 2px rounded white outline.

## Shapes

The shape system embraces extreme curvature (`level 3` - Pill-shaped), providing an environment safe from sharp corners and technical coldness.

### Corner Curvature Rules
- **Interactive Chips, Tags, Badges & Primary Buttons:** Full circular pill rounding (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Cards & Data Modules:** Hyper-rounded rectangles (`rounded-2xl` ~ `20px` to `24px`).
- **Avatar Frames & Metric Badges:** Squircle and wobbly cloud geometry (asymmetrical rounded corners such as `28px 18px 26px 20px`) to inject mascot-like handcrafted charm.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Sunburst Action"):** Background rich golden amber `#F59E0B`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill (`9999px`), bold Quicksand font. Bottom-edge tactile bevel: `border-b-4 border-[#D97706]`. Active state translates `translate-y-[2px]` and reduces the border to 2px.
- **Secondary ("Strawberry Gentle"):** Background `#FFF1F2`, text `#FB7185`, hover background `#FFE4E6`, border `1.5px solid #FECDD3`.
- **Icon Stamp Buttons:** Circular white buttons with a subtle tinted pastel background for icons like stars, suns, pencils, and hearts.

### Chips & Badges
- **Sticker Badges:** Rounded pill tags featuring a tiny companion icon (seedling, sun, pencil).
  - *Completed / Excellent:* Mint `#ECFDF5` background with `#047857` text and `#A7F3D0` soft border.
  - *In Progress / Homework:* Custard `#FEF3C7` background with `#B45309` text.
  - *Needs Help / Attention:* Peach Pink `#FFF1F2` background with `#BE123C` text.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Input Fields:** Generously padded (14px vertical, 18px horizontal), `rounded-2xl`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #F5EBE1`. On focus: border shifts to `#F59E0B` accompanied by a soft halo ring `0 0 0 4px rgba(254, 243, 199, 0.65)`.
- **Checkboxes:** Chunky rounded squares (`rounded-lg`, 22px × 22px) with a soft 2px border. Checked state fills with `#10B981` revealing a white rounded checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Smooth pill disks with concentric warm butter dot fills.

### Cards & Dashboards
- **Learning Milestone Cards:** Pure white containers wrapped in a 1.5px `#FEEBC8` border with a 24px corner radius. Include playful header zones with pastel color-block ribbons or sticker badges pinned to the top right corner.
- **Playful Progress Trackers:** Thick 12px pill-shaped progress bars filled with soft multi-color gradients (amber to mint) topped with a smiling star indicator at the current progress position.
