## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intelligent, serene, and pedagogically sound digital workspace for monitoring academic growth, parental supervision, and tutor collaboration. The personality pivots from warm pink-coral to an intellectual, gentle blue-sky palette that projects calm clarity, reliability, and cognitive ease during lengthy supervisory sessions.

The design philosophy combines **Clean Institutional Minimalism** with **Light Glassmorphic Nuances**:
- **Atmosphere:** Soothing, orderly, and transparent. The interface substitutes high-stress visual alarms with structured blue-sky hierarchies that comfort educators and parents while preserving high analytical precision.
- **Surface Texture:** Pristine white cards floating upon delicate sky-tinted canvasses (`#F0F7FF`, `#E0F2FE`), complemented by translucent frosted navigation panes and soft cyan-tinted borders.
- **Cultural & Linguistic Optimization:** Tuned specifically for Vietnamese diacritics with generous baseline clearance, ensuring tone marks (*sắc, huyền, hỏi, ngã, nặng*) remain unclipped and visually balanced in high-density data views.

## Colors

The color palette centers on tranquil, reliable sky blues, azure primaries, and deep oceanic navy for maximum cognitive clarity and contrast.

### Core Roles
- **Primary Electric Ocean (`#2563EB`):** Main interactive triggers, focused controls, active navigation nodes, and primary system metrics. Deep hover state transitions to `#1D4ED8`.
- **Secondary Cyan Sky (`#0284C7`):** Secondary pathways, auxiliary schedules, assignment trackers, and informative chart curves.
- **Tertiary Teal Mint (`#0D9488`):** Verified academic milestones, positive study habits, and completed session markers.
- **Neutral Slate (`#64748B`):** Structural borders, secondary text lines, breadcrumbs, and muted table columns.

### Specialized Surfaces & Accents
- **Canvas (`sky-lightest` / `#F0F7FF`):** Soft, cool background with delicate blue undertones that reduces eye strain compared to blinding pure white.
- **Container Accent (`sky-soft` / `#E0F2FE`):** Highlight fills, table hover backgrounds, and selected filter states.
- **High-Contrast Text (`navy-dark` / `#0F172A`):** Deep navy tone delivering optimal WCAG AAA readability for student scores, timesheets, and headings.
- **Surface Container Base:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, modal sheets, and data tables.

## Typography

The typography continues using **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels. Its balanced geometric proportions and ample vertical heights prevent Vietnamese diacritical collision while projecting a friendly, pedagogical tone.

- **Display & Headings:** Dense letter-spacing (`-0.025em` to `-0.01em`) and solid weights (600–800) create clear visual anchoring on student reports and dashboard summaries.
- **Body & Numerical Metrics:** Line heights are configured at 1.5x minimum to ensure multi-line tutor comments and teacher notes retain clean reading rhythm. Numerical scores use tabular figures.
- **PIN & Security Locks (`code-md`):** Extended tracking (`0.15em`) maintains distinct digit separation for PIN inputs, session locks, and parental override interfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a fluid desktop workspace running a responsive 12-column grid:

- **Sidebar Navigation:** Fixed at `264px` on desktop, framing the left boundary of the administrative workspace.
- **Main Canvas:** Dynamic fluid area bounded by `margin` (`2rem`) padding on desktop monitors, reducing to `margin-compact` (`1.25rem`) on tablets.
- **Grid Gutter:** Standard views utilize `gutter` (`1.5rem`), while data-dense student logs shift to `gutter-dense` (`1rem`).
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Component interiors employ `space-sm` (`0.5rem`) for tightly connected elements (labels to inputs) and `space-md` (`1rem`) to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) between operational sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses cool, tinted tonal layers and subtle ambient shadows to avoid muddy grays:

- **Surface Base (Level 0):** Flat `#F0F7FF` canvas, providing a soft, soothing architectural floor.
- **Surface Cards (Level 1):** Solid white `#FFFFFF` bounded by a crisp 1px stroke of `#E0F2FE` (or `#CBD5E1` on high-contrast cards). Ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Frosted Analytical Containers (Level 1 Glass):** Card background of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlines use `1px solid rgba(224, 242, 254, 0.9)`.
- **Flyouts, Dropdowns & Tooltips (Level 2):** Elevated pure white containers casting ambient navy-blue shadows: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.06)`.
- **Modals & Verification Sheets (Level 3):** Floated centrally over a tinted backdrop curtain (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`). Shadow: `0 24px 38px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains level 2 roundedness to harmonize friendly education warmth with administrative structure:

- **Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) for ergonomic balance.
- **Panels, Cards, and Modals:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to produce soft, modern macro frames.
- **Chips, Badges, and Filter Pills:** Full pill (`9999px`) shapes that clearly differentiate informational tags from rectangular inputs.
- **PIN Verification Inputs:** `0.75rem` (`12px`) squares with balanced corner smoothing.

## Components

### 1. Navigation Sidebar
- **Dimensions & Background:** Fixed width `264px`, full-height sticky. Frosted pure white surface with a right-hand hairline border in `#E0F2FE`.
- **Student Profile Switcher:** Styled dropdown module at the top (`Học sinh: Gia Bảo - Lớp 4`) backed by `#F0F7FF` with a subtle `#BAE6FD` outline.
- **Menu Items:** Inactive items display in `#475569`. Hovering shifts to `bg-sky-50 text-blue-700`. Active state features a 3px vertical accent bar in `#2563EB`, background fill `#EFF6FF`, bold weighting (`label-lg`), and an ocean blue icon.

### 2. Glassmorphic Metric Cards
- **Surfaces:** Elevated Level 1 Glass surface, `rounded-lg`, interior padding `1.25rem`.
- **Arrangement:** Top row presents the category title (`label-md`, uppercase, `#64748B`) alongside a status pill. Center displays large numeric KPIs (`display-sm`, `#0F172A`). The bottom line shows the comparative progress delta (e.g., `+15% so với tuần trước` styled in `#059669`).

### 3. High-Density Data Tables
- **Table Card:** Enclosed in `#FFFFFF`, perimeter stroke 1px `#E2E8F0`, `rounded-lg`.
- **Header:** Background `#F0F7FF`, typography `label-sm`, tracking uppercase, text `#334155`.
- **Data Rows:** Height 52px (standard) or 40px (compact logs). Subtle border-b `#F1F5F9`. Hover state illuminates the row with `#F8FAFC` or `#F0F7FF` at 50% opacity.
- **Student Identity Cell:** Displays an avatar circle alongside the student's full name, class tier, and subject tag.

### 4. Interactive PIN Entry & Keypad
- **Purpose:** Parental controls, curriculum unlocking, and study session verifications.
- **Indicators:** 4 to 6 dot slots. Empty slots display a 2px stroke in `#BAE6FD`; filled slots transition with a spring pulse into vibrant `#2563EB`.
- **Keypad Matrix:** 3x4 grid composed of squircle buttons (`64px x 64px`). Default surface `#FFFFFF` with border `#E0F2FE`; hover activates `#F0F7FF` and active press renders `#BAE6FD`.

### 5. Status Badges & Academic Chips
- **Đang học (In Progress):** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1D4ED8`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`.
- **Hoàn thành (Completed):** Background `#F0FDF4`, text `#047857`, border `1px solid #BBF7D0`.
- **Cần nhắc nhở (Attention):** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
- **Chưa nộp bài (Overdue):** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#B91C1C`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.

### 6. Search & Filters
- **Search Bar:** Left-aligned magnifying glass icon. Pure white surface with `#CBD5E1` border. Focus state elevates with border `#2563EB` and focus ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped capsules. Inactive chips use `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` border. Active chips switch to `#2563EB` fill with crisp white text.

### 7. Action Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#2563EB`, hover `#1D4ED8`, active `#1E40AF`, text `#FFFFFF`. Shadow: `0 2px 4px 0 rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#1E293B`, hover `bg-sky-50/50 border-sky-300`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#2563EB`, hover `bg-blue-50`.
